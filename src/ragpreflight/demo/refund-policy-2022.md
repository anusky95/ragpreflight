# Fares and Refunds Policy

SkyFare Air wants every customer to understand how fares, changes and refunds work before they book. This policy applies to tickets purchased on skyfare-air.com, through the SkyFare app, or by phone. Fares are priced per passenger and include taxes and carrier-imposed fees shown at checkout. Basic fares cannot be changed after the 24-hour cancellation window closes, while Standard and Flex fares can be changed online for the fare difference. Flex fares may also be refunded to the original form of payment if the request is made at least two hours before departure. ## Bereavement fares

Bereavement fares are offered to immediate family members travelling because of a death in the family.
Customers may apply for the reduced bereavement fare up to 90 days after travel by submitting the online request form with a death certificate.
Approved refunds are processed within 30 days of the request.
